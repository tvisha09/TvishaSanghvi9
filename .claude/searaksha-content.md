## Slide 5 — 8OjJBNGWSLaIOLvrhNZtbG0bH0.png
### Type
research
### Title
Secondary gaps
### Body text
Five outlined cards in a 3 + 2 grid (top row: 3 cards; bottom row: 2 cards, left-aligned):

1. **Inadequate Training & Digital Literacy** — Even if tools exist (mobile apps, digital fish finders), many fishermen lack confidence, literacy, or familiarity.
2. **Lack of Accurate & Localized Data on Fishing Grounds** — Most small-scale fishermen rely on generational knowledge, signs like bird movement, or trial-and-error to locate fish. Existing scientific fish stock data is either not granular, not updated in real-time, or not accessible in simple, local language formats.
3. **Supply Chain & Market Data Blind Spots** — Fishermen often don't know real-time market prices or demand trends, rely on middlemen who underpay.
4. **No Contextual Weather & Sea Condition Information** — Weather forecasts are either too broad (city-level) or too technical (wave height, currents). No integration of real-time micro-weather + sea state + local knowledge to give personalized warnings.
5. **Lack of Safety & Rescue Networks** — No easy distress communication tools. Most small boats lack VHF radios or GPS beacons. Rescue coordination is fragmented between marine police, coast guard, and informal community alerts.

Data flow
### Body text
GPS & Location Data
- Sources:
  - GNSS (Global Navigation Satellite System: GPS, GLONASS, NavIC)
  - Phone-based or external GPS antenna
- Flow:
  - GPS Satellites → Device → Fisherman (real-time map/position)
  - Device → Base Station → Family/Dock for live tracking

Emergency Distress Signals
- Sources:
  - Fisherman manually triggers SOS
  - Automatic triggers (capsize, high impact, engine stall) from onboard sensors
- Flow:
  - Device → Satellite or Cellular Network → Coast Guard / Navy / Marine Police → Rescue Team Dispatch
  - Optional: P2P signal → Nearby Boats for quick response

Fish Stock & Sea Condition Data
- Sources:
  - Government Fisheries Department
  - Scientific Fish Stock Databases
  - Community Shared Data (Catch Density via Cooperative Inputs)
- Flow:
  - Database → Cloud → Device (via lightweight API) → Fisherman (via iconographic/simple display)
